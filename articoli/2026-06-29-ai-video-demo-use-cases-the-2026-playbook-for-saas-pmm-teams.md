The website hero embed is the highest-traffic AI video demo use case for any SaaS PMM team. A 30-60 second autoplay product video sitting above the fold replaces the static screenshot and the "watch video" modal that prospects skip.

This lane works because the buyer in 2026 is already scanning for proof-of-product on the homepage — Consensus 2026 data shows buyers consume product evidence well before reaching a rep. Sales follow-up videos are the second-most-cited AI video demo use case from PMM teams enabling sales. The 60-90 second format sent within 24 hours of a discovery call repeats the prospect's stated use case back as a personalized product walkthrough — without the rep recording from scratch.

Customer onboarding walkthroughs are 2-3 minute step-by-step videos that ship inside the welcome email or activation flow. The format works because new users learning a product on day one will not read a 12-step doc — they will watch a video.

This use case maps directly to activation rate, which most SaaS PMM teams measure as percent of new accounts reaching the first value milestone within 14 days. Paid social ads are 15-30 second AI-generated demo examples optimized for LinkedIn and X feed placement. The format trades depth for thumb-stopping clarity — the first 3 seconds need to communicate the product category and the value prop without sound.

Partner enablement reels are 90-second AI video walkthroughs sent to channel partners for cobranded selling. The partner needs to understand the product well enough to sell it without ever opening it — a video walkthrough collapses the ramp time from weeks to one viewing session.
