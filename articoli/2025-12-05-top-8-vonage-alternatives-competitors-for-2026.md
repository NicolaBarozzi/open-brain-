## Why Businesses Are Looking for Vonage Alternatives?

Many businesses are wondering if Vonage can work with the times in terms of automation, AI, and unified workflows. Demand for tools of integrated communication is growing rapidly. In fact, the market for UCaaS is expected to increase from USD 54.9 billion in 2022 to over USD 311.6 billion by 2032, which is a clear indication of increased adoption of more robust platforms.

### 1. Lack of Depth in Offered Features

Vonage does well for basic calling and conferencing, but it does not have the deeper artificial intelligence-based call analytics, robust omnichannel operations, and smarter automation functionality. Businesses now want platforms that combine voice, messaging, AI tools, and customer engagement under one roof.

### 2. Hidden or Add-On Fees

On Vonage, many core features, like a virtual receptionist or certain types of analytics, can cost extra. They may hike up monthly costs and drive businesses to VoIP providers who offer more transparent, all-inclusive pricing.

### 3. UI/UX Challenges

The Vonage app can sometimes be less user-friendly than contemporary VoIP platforms. Working with extensions, call flows, and settings can be sluggish, especially for small teams that need fast navigation.

### 4. Customer Support Concerns

A few companies mention that Vonage offered a lack of customer support during onboarding. That drawback has led companies to look for Vonage business alternatives with more dependable, around-the-clock service.

### 5. Rising Demand for Unified Communication Features

Teams want calling, video, messaging, and collaboration in one dashboard. Vonage is not quite a one-stop shop, making some companies look at other platforms to fit their all-in-one plan.

- Vonage is a cloud phone service that delivers VoIP phone system functionality through internet connectivity. Businesses use it to manage phone calls, video meetings, messaging, and contact center solutions.
- It’s designed to help teams communicate more efficiently with features like an AI virtual assistant, conference bridge, CRM integrations, and more. Vonage supports remote teams, simplifies customer interactions, and provides a unified platform for all business communication needs.

## How Do You Switch to Vonage Alternative?

It is not difficult to port from Vonage to its alternative if you do it in a systematic way. Here’s what you need to do to change providers without affecting your business.

**1. Start Number Porting First: **Begin by submitting a number porting request with your new VoIP provider. Keep your Vonage account active during this stage, because your number must remain live for the port to be approved.

**2. Export Your Data: **Download phone logs, voicemails, and settings, then upload to the chosen VoIP provider’s system to prevent re-entering the data.

**3. Configure Your New VoIP System: **Set up your call flows, IVR menu, voicemail, extensions, routing rules, and CRM or helpdesk integrations. Align it with your existing workflow so the transition feels seamless to your team.

**4. Test Calls Across Devices:** Check call quality, routing logic, voicemail transcription, and performance of the desktop app and mobile phone app as well.

**5. Close Your Vonage Account:** Once everything’s up and running smoothly, cancel the subscription of the Vonage account so you do not end up with overlapping billing.
