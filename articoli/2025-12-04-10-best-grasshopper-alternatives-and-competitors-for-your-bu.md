## Why Businesses Look for Grasshopper Alternatives?

Using Grasshopper can restrict your business in more than one way. Once businesses start to grow, it becomes inevitable for them to start looking for Grasshopper alternatives. Here are the reasons why businesses start looking for one.

### 1. Limited Advanced Features

If you are a solopreneur, Grasshopper might be a decent choice for you. But it is quite important to note that once your team grows, you will face a lot of limitations with Grasshopper. Grasshopper does not offer many advanced features that are must-haves to manage calls efficiently.

Call queues, call routing, detailed analytics, and call recording, Grasshopper offers none of these. Having limited advanced features is one of the major reasons businesses look for Grasshopper competitors. A competitor that provides essential features that prove to be productive for the teams.

### 2. Weak Team Collaboration Capabilities

Lack of internal collaboration capabilities is another major drawback for businesses using Grasshopper. It lacks shared inboxes, team messaging, shared call notes, etc. Businesses need these features for quick coordination and faster response times.

For a team with multiple team members, poor visibility can cause chaos. Modern systems are built to solve such issues. They offer,

- Shared call history
- Call tagging
- Voicemail sharing
- Easy transfer options

This helps teams to collaborate flawlessly. Such a collaboration-friendly environment is what pushes businesses towards searching for great alternatives to Grasshopper.

### 3. Outdated Interface Compared to Modern VoIP Tools

Businesses use VoIP platforms to ease their workload a little. Does it make sense to use one if it feels outdated? This is what Grasshopper offers: an outdated interface. Its app lacks the sleek and modern workflows.

The outdated UI slows teams down, as it increases the time to adapt. Modern business phone systems aim for speed and clarity. They provide easy-to-set-up drag-and-drop call flows. These modern platforms are built to enhance teamwork. Therefore, there is no other option for businesses other than to look for a Grasshopper alternative if they want a more modern and responsive platform.

### 4. Pricing Concerns for Growing Teams

Businesses need to understand that cheap is not equivalent to good. Grasshopper’s base pricing plan might sound attractive, but it can cost businesses more in the long run. The reason is that, with growing requirements, the cost grows as well and is hard to justify.

The pricing can put a dent in your business’s budget if you require multiple numbers. And do not forget, even after paying big, you still do not get the must-have advanced features. That’s why many businesses are moving towards services like Grasshopper that consist of more and better features.

## Common Problems Users Face After Switching From Grasshopper (And How to Avoid Them)

Switching to a Grasshopper alternative is not the only thing you need to worry about. You need to stay proactive in order to avoid mishaps after switching. Here’s what can go wrong and how you can avoid it.

### 1. Confusion During Number Porting

Many users worry about losing their number during the switch. Porting delays or missing documentation often cause confusion.  **How to avoid it:**

- Submit accurate documents for faster and flawless completion of the number porting process.
- Confirm eligibility with the carrier before starting the process.
- Schedule porting during off-hours; this ensures the operation remains intact.
- Use temporary call forwarding so that no calls go answered.

### 2. Losing Old Voicemails or Call History

Old voicemails or call history might have some crucial information. For businesses, it can be a point of concern if they lose them. Not all providers automatically migrate voicemail data. The process may cause some friction.

**How to avoid it:**

- Export all voicemails before migrating.
- Take screenshots or download logs to keep track of the data.
- Ask the new provider about migration tools.

### 3. New Phone System Feeling “Too Complicated”

Sometimes, adapting to a new system can feel a little overwhelming to some teams. An advanced system with new features can feel unfamiliar or too complicated.

**How to avoid it:**

- Provide short training sessions to your teams so that they can use the system to its full potential.
- Use onboarding tutorials to fast-track and make the training process easy.
- Create cheat sheets for your team so that they can use them whenever they are stuck.
- Configure default settings for everyone.

### 4. Team Members Not Understanding New Tools

New apps, AI features, and routing flows can confuse staff. Adoption takes time. Sometimes it can even get overwhelming.

**How to avoid it:**

- Offer in-app training to let team members understand the system better.
- Assign champions in each department to keep each and every team member motivated.

### 5. Improper Call Routing Setup

When switching to a new system, it is a must to ensure the routing works effectively. Improper routing can lead to unnecessary delays and customer frustration.

**How to avoid it:**

- Testing is equally important as adopting a new system. Therefore, test routing before going live
- Keep IVR instructions short; this way, the risk of errors is reduced.
- Add fallback routing for missed calls.

### 6. Missing Integrations Right After Switching

Teams expect everything to sync instantly. This might not be possible each time. Missing CRM integrations can slow down the operational workflows.

**How to avoid it:**

- Connect CRM before launch; this proactive approach will help you keep going with the same productivity, even when switching to a new system.
- Test call logging in advance to confirm that the system works smoothly.
