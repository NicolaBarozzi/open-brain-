- **Policy Shift**: The Trump administration's June 22 executive orders represent a significant policy shift, expanding federal focus on quantum computing beyond basic research to commercialization, deployment, and national security applications, highlighting its strategic importance.
- **National Quantum Strategy Update**: One executive order mandates an update to the National Quantum Strategy within 180 days, prioritizing domestic manufacturing, workforce development, and public-private partnerships to drive the commercialization of quantum technologies and enhance U.S. economic competitiveness.
- **Investment Opportunities**: With increasing federal support for the quantum ecosystem, investors should pay attention to companies like QUBT and IBM, which are well-positioned to benefit from the growing market demand driven by these policy changes in quantum technology commercialization and deployment.
- **IBM's Investment Commitment**: IBM has committed to investing over $10 billion in the next five years to accelerate quantum research and manufacturing, aiming for the first demonstrations of quantum advantage by 2026, aligning closely with the administration's focus on domestic manufacturing and commercialization, thereby strengthening its market position.

## About IBM

International Business Machines Corporation is a provider of global hybrid cloud and artificial intelligence (AI) and consulting expertise. The Company’s segments include Software, Consulting, Infrastructure and Financing. The Software segment includes hybrid cloud and AI platforms, which allow clients to realize their digital and AI transformations across the applications, data, and environments in which they operate. The Consulting segment focuses on integrating skills on strategy, experience, technology and operations by domain and industry. The Infrastructure segment is focused on the hybrid cloud infrastructure market, providing on-premises and cloud-based server and storage solutions. In addition, it offers a portfolio of life-cycle services for hybrid cloud infrastructure deployment. The Financing segment provides client and commercial financing, facilitating its clients’ acquisition of hardware, software and services. It helps clients in more than 175 countries.
