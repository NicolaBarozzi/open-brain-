What 48 Million Phone Calls Reveal About the Customer Experience at Car Dealerships More than 48 million incoming calls across thousands of dealerships were analysed by AutoSuccess, one of the leading US publications for the automotive retail industry. Two trends stood out immediately: Low answer rates and long waiting times remain two of the biggest risks in automotive retail. During the second half of 2025, the average dealership successfully connected only 65% of incoming calls to a qualified member of staff. For busy dealerships, this means that hundreds—or even thousands—of potential customers never reach the right person. Why do so many customers fail to reach the right person? They hang up while waiting in the queue. They’re diverted to voicemail. A message is taken instead of resolving the enquiry immediately. In every one of these situations, the dealership loses control of the customer experience. And with outbound callback success rates averaging only around 20%, many customers simply never try again. The real cost isn’t the missed call. It’s the lost customer. Research by Talkdesk, MetricNet and Zendesk also shows that caller abandonment increases significantly after just 30 seconds on hold. Why Many Dealerships Still Don’t Act Surprisingly, many dealership managers either underestimate the problem or simply fail to recognise it. Only 25% of dealership decision-makers consider telephone availability to be a significant operational challenge. Yet every unanswered call represents far more than a missed opportunity. It sends a clear message to the customer: “Your enquiry isn’t important enough for us right now.” Telephone communication may be one of the oldest customer channels. Yet it remains one of the most valuable sources of new business. High availability is no longer just good customer service—it’s a genuine competitive advantage. #AI #Carla #Phone #CarDealer
