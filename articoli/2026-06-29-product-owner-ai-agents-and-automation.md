# Product Owner, AI Agents and Automation

**Why**__Jerry.ai__**?**- Join a pre-IPO startup with capital ($240M raised), traction (5M+ customers), and runway (we’re profitable). 
- Impact: Come lead our customer-facing Agentic AI product leveraging LLMs, agents, and internal APIs to create a magical experience for our customers. Work with partners like OpenAI to integrate - __Jerry.ai__services directly into the ChatGPT App and pioneer new model capabilities.
- Accelerated career growth. We have a flat org chart, ambitious goals, and no shortage of opportunities for people who want more. You’ll also report directly to our COO, leading one of our most important growth levers. 


**Why we’re hiring this role:**Your car and your home are your most important assets, yet the experience of owning one is stuck in the 90s. Every part of the journey — buy/sell, insurance, maintenance, repairs — is fragmented, complicated, and expensive. Jerry.ai is building the first app to manage it all.

We launched car insurance in 2019 and became one of the top three brokers in the country. We got there in part because we were early on generative AI. Before off-the-shelf platforms existed, we built our own infrastructure to automate sales and service conversations. Today, that system handles a meaningful share of customer interactions end-to-end. The underlying technology is entirely homegrown.

That head start created real leverage. It also created real complexity. Prompts that power everything from the customer-facing chatbot to vehicle photo validation to back-office automation now live across six separate locations. There is no single source of truth, no unified platform, and no clear owner of where the next big bets — voicebot, computer use, consumer AI applications — belong on the roadmap. That is what you will own.


**How you’ll make an impact:**You will sit at the intersection of product, engineering, and applied AI as you build and scale a sophisticated system that already automates >70% of inbound sales and service requests (over 50k chats per month). Your role will include shaping how modern LLM systems, human-in-the-loop feedback, and computer-use agents redefine an entire industry. Specifically:

- Lead end-to-end development for our AI platform and customer experiences, from initial roadmap to rollout. 
- Partner with engineers to design prompt strategies, evaluation frameworks, and guardrails—balancing latency, cost, and accuracy. 
- Serve as the technical translator between engineering and the broader organization, establishing AI best practices and platform standards. 
- Drive systematic improvement in answer quality, customer satisfaction, and automation rates through rigorous experimentation. 
- Work with partners at OpenAI to evaluate and deploy the next generation of voice models and workflow automation. 


**Who you are:**- **Technologist:**You live and breathe AI. You read the release notes when a new model drops and you’ve already built your own custom workflows.
- **Systems Thinker:**You are comfortable diving into technical conversations about API design and system architecture, translating complex concepts for any audience.
- **Experimenter:**You think only in terms of what’s possible. AI is rapidly evolving, and you’re constantly tinkering and finding new capabilities that didn’t exist yesterday.
- **Data-Driven:**You are familiar with SQL and comfortable diving into the data to answer your own questions and validate hypotheses.
- **Pragmatic:**You can navigate technical tradeoffs and lead by example when implementing AI platform standards.


**Ideal experience:**- 3+ years of experience in a technical role like forward deployed engineering, technical product management, or similar at a fast-paced startup. 
- Passion and curiosity for AI systems including modern LLMs, etc. 
- Track record of taking complex, strategic ideas and turning them into scalable, production-grade products.
