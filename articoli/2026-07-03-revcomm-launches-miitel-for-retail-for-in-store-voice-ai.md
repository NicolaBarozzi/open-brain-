# RevComm launches MiiTel for Retail for in-store voice AI

### KEY POINTS

- RevComm launches MiiTel for Retail to record and analyze in-person conversations at stores, counters and field sites
- Service combines face-to-face interactions with phone and web meeting analysis on a single MiiTel dashboard
- Target uses include service quality improvement, customer harassment detection, CRM linkage and voice-of-customer analysis

RevComm has launched MiiTel for Retail, a new voice AI service that records and analyzes face-to-face conversations at stores, service counters and field sites, the Tokyo-based company said on July 2.

The service is designed to turn in-person customer interactions, a major but often under-digitized customer touchpoint, into data that companies can use for service quality improvement, frontline support, customer harassment countermeasures, voice-of-customer analysis and management decisions.

MiiTel for Retail records store-floor conversations in real time and uses AI to analyze them alongside phone calls and web meetings on a unified platform. The service includes real-time monitoring, real-time response support, quality evaluation and automatic linkage with customer relationship management systems, managed through the MiiTel dashboard.

RevComm said the product can detect customer harassment during in-store interactions in real time while helping companies visualize and structure customer-service know-how and customer feedback that had previously accumulated informally at individual locations. It is targeting customer-facing operations in sectors including finance, real estate, automobiles, insurance, retail and services.

The launch extends RevComm's MiiTel lineup, which started with the MiiTel Phone call analysis service in October 2018. The company later added MiiTel Call Center, MiiTel Meetings and the in-person conversation analysis tool MiiTel RecPod, and in 2026 began offering the generative AI solution MiiTel Synapse.

With the new service, RevComm said it has completed the voice platform it has aimed to build since its founding, enabling companies to centrally store, manage and analyze customer and internal conversations across phone, online and in-person settings on a single platform.

RevComm, headquartered in Tokyo's Chiyoda Ward, develops AI- and voice-based software and databases. The company was the only Asian company selected for Forbes' AI 50 list in 2023, won an innovation award in the AI category at CES 2025, and received the Minister for Internal Affairs and Communications Award at the Japan Startup Awards 2025.
