# New Dynamics Group Launches AI-Powered Digital Marketing Agency for Local Businesses

**CENTERVILLE, OH - MAY 23, 2026 - **New Dynamics Group, Inc., a Centerville, Ohio-based AI-powered digital marketing agency, today announced the launch of its precision marketing platform for local businesses across the United States. The platform delivers fully done-for-you local SEO, Google Ads management, Google Business Profile optimization, reputation management, AI voice bots, and lead tracking — all executed autonomously under a transparent, results-first model.

What separates New Dynamics Group from traditional digital marketing agencies is its zero-risk entry model. Every engagement begins with a free, five-minute business analysis that evaluates over 500 data points — including local search rankings, competitor ad spend, Google Business Profile completeness, and speed-to-lead performance. The AI platform then generates a complete 12-month growth plan with month-by-month revenue projections and cost breakdowns. Business owners see the full plan before committing to anything.

The agency serves high-value local service markets including dental practices, HVAC contractors, law firms, med spas, auto repair shops, and real estate agents, etc — industries where the lifetime value of a single new client routinely exceeds $10,000 and where digital visibility directly determines growth.

*“Local business owners deserve the same AI marketing intelligence that enterprise brands have access to — without the complexity or the guesswork. We show every client exactly what they will achieve, in real numbers, before they spend a single dollar. That level of transparency is the foundation everything we do is built on.” ***— Founder, New Dynamics Group, Inc.**

The platform’s capabilities include Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — positioning clients to be recommended by AI-powered search platforms including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity — alongside 24/7 AI chat and voice bot implementation, Google Local Services Ads management, Facebook and Instagram advertising, automated review generation, and full-stack call tracking with attribution reporting.

New Dynamics Group is currently accepting new clients. Local businesses interested in a free gap analysis and 12-month growth plan can visit **www.newdynamicsgroup.com** or email **newdynamicsgroup@gmail.com.**
