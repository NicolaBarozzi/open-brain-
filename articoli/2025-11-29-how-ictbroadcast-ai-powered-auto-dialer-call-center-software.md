## How ICTBroadcast Transforms Auto Dialing

### 1. AI-Powered Predictive Dialing

One of ICTBroadcast’s standout features is its AI-powered predictive dialing. Predictive dialing is a method of automatically dialing numbers before an agent is available, based on statistical analysis of agent availability and call duration. ICTBroadcast uses AI to predict when an agent will be free to take the next call, minimizing idle time and maximizing talk time. This not only increases agent productivity but also ensures customers are connected to an agent almost immediately.

The AI-driven algorithm continuously learns from call data to improve dialing accuracy. By eliminating wasted calls to busy signals, answering machines, or disconnected numbers, the AI ensures that agents are only connected to live prospects. This feature significantly boosts the efficiency of outbound campaigns, whether for sales, fundraising, or customer surveys.

### 2. Power Dialing and Progressive Dialing

While predictive dialing is ideal for high-volume campaigns, ICTBroadcast also offers power dialing and progressive dialing modes for more controlled environments. Power dialing automatically dials the next number once an agent is free, ensuring a continuous workflow. Progressive dialing takes a more measured approach, only dialing one number at a time and waiting for an agent to complete their current call before initiating the next one.

These dialing modes offer businesses flexibility based on the nature of their campaigns. For smaller teams or more personalized outreach, progressive dialing can maintain quality interactions. In contrast, power dialing can be used to rapidly connect agents with customers when speed is a priority.
