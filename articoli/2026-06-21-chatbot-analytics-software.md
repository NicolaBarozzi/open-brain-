Chatbot analytics software collects, analyzes, and visualizes data from all of your chatbots’ interactions with customers. Verint Bot Analytics is a chatbot analytics platform that captures interaction data from all of your bots – across voice and digital channels – and delivers all of the tools you need to understand your bot performance and quickly take action to improve it.

The most impactful chatbot KPIs fall into three categories: automation, experience, and cost. Bot Automation measures how often your bot fully resolves a customer’s issue without escalating to a live agent. Bot Experience captures how customers feel after interacting with your bot. Cost Per Automated Chat quantifies the value your bot is generating. Verint Bot Analytics surfaces all three as cornerstone KPIs in a single dashboard, so you always know where you stand.

Bot containment rate is the percentage of customer conversations your bot resolves without transferring to a live agent. It’s one of the clearest indicators of bot effectiveness and one of the most direct levers for reducing contact center costs. A higher containment rate means fewer agent-handled conversations, shorter queues, and more capacity for complex issues. Verint Bot Analytics helps you monitor containment in real time, identify why conversations are escalating, and take targeted action to improve it.

When bots perform better, agents are freed up to focus on higher-value work. Verint Bot Analytics gives teams the visibility to identify exactly where bots are falling short – whether that’s unanswered messages, misconfigured intents, or content gaps – and fix those issues quickly. The result is more conversations resolved automatically, less escalation traffic reaching your agents, and a team that can handle greater volume without increasing headcount.
