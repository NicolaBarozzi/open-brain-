## Real Results from Two Leading Brands

A global beverage group operating in over 40 countries faced rising labor costs and slow service response. After deploying an all-media smart service center with intelligent IVR and smart ticketing, the results were clear:

- Smart IVR now auto-answers over 80 percent of repetitive inquiries

- Manual labor costs dropped by more than 70 percent

- The effective resolution rate rose by 15 percent

- Internal task handling efficiency improved by over 50 percent

 

 

A leading energy drink brand struggled with fragmented store communication. Store owners had to navigate multiple systems, causing delays in order processing. The brand deployed a smart voice bot solution. Intelligent voice calls now remind terminal stores about replenishment needs automatically, with SMS notifications sent simultaneously. The system records store feedback in real time, giving headquarters full visibility. Customer satisfaction improved by 80 percent.

## Why an Intelligent Call Center Matters

Both brands faced the same pressure: standing out in saturated overseas markets where local players have home-court advantage. The solution wasn't a marketing campaign. It was operational efficiency. Faster response, consistent service, and real-time store communication build trust. That trust drives repeat orders and better shelf placement.

 

An intelligent cloud call center unifies phone, email, chat, and social channels into one agent workspace. Smart routing ensures the right agent handles each inquiry. Cloud-based deployment means brands can launch service in new countries within hours.
